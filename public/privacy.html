Privacy policy

Student Icon · last updated 25 November 2026

What this site is

Student Icon and its Pixel Pals Arcade are small browser games and a player-profile demo. You can play every game without an account. Nothing here is sold, and no profile is built about you for our own use.

Cookies and browser storage

This site does not set its own tracking cookies. It does use your browser's local storage, which stays on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Game records — your best scores in the arcade are stored in this browser so they survive a reload.
  • Session — if you sign in, the session token is kept in local storage so you stay signed in on that device. Signing out removes it.

Clearing site data in your browser erases both, along with your local game records.

Google AdSense and third-party cookies

This site shows advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites, and to measure how ads perform.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and others.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised ads at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are served, and can change that choice later.

Accounts and profile data

Creating a player is optional. If you do, we store the email address and profile details you type in (handle, display name, focus) with our authentication and database provider, Supabase, so you can sign in again and other players can see your public profile. We do not sell or rent that data. Ask us to delete your player and we will remove the account and its profile.

Server logs

Like most websites, the host serving these pages records standard request logs — IP address, browser type, page requested and time — for security and to keep the site running.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions about this policy or a request to delete your data can be sent through the project's issue tracker at github.com/shannu760/arcadelabs.
